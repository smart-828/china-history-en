China through time

Chinese history from the first farming villages to today, told in ten eras for readers starting from scratch, with maps, charts, daily life, key people, common myths, how historians know what they know, and what was happening in Europe and the world at the same moment.

Three threads run through all ten eras: the cycle of unity and division, the meeting of farming China with the peoples of the northern grasslands, and China's contact with the wider world. Watch for them.
